02 / TRANSFORMATION
How adjacent substeps work

ON: adjacent selected operations use equal subintervals, least changed footprint area first. OFF: in each adjacent group, shared winners take priority, then greatest changed footprint area; successful winners defer their neighbours. Other non-adjacent selections also run, each over the full interval. A shared winner touches at least two selected neighbours and beats at least one by changed area. Failed candidates are reported and the next candidate is tried.
